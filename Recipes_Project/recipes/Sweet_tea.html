Sweet lemon tea

Description

Great energizing drink for any time of the day, any weather. Goes particulary well with a buterbrod

Ingredients

  • Boiling water
  • Black tea - 1 bag
  • Lemon wedge - 1 piece
  • Sugar - 2 teaspoons

Instructions

  1. Boil some water in the kettle
  2. Pour water over the bag in a cup
  3. Add the sugar and the lemon
