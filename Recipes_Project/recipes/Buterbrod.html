Buterbrod with doctors sausage

Description

Eternal classic of single men and gaming night. Pairs well with sweet black tea with lemon

Ingredients

  • White bread "Baton" - 1 slice
  • Sausage "Doktorskaya" - 2 slices

Instructions

  1. Cut a slice of bread.
  2. Cut two formidable slices of the sausage
  3. Put the sausage on the bread overlapping the splices of the sausage. Serve with sweat tea
