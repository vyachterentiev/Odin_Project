Classic Medovik Recipe

Description

The classic Medovik cake was once invented by a young court chef for Empress Elizaveta Alexeyevna, the wife of Alexander I. Interestingly, she did not like honey, yet in this form she simply “didn’t recognize” it and ordered the cake to be prepared regularly. When the truth was eventually revealed, the empress did not revoke her decision but merely laughed at the chef’s boldness. Thus, Medovik began to win the hearts of sweet-toothed dessert lovers and, it should be noted, continues to do so to this day.

The cake layers turn out very tender and crumbly, while the pleasant tang of the sour-cream frosting gently balances their cloying sweetness. Medovik is loved by many, but not everyone knows how to make it at home. Well then, it’s time to fix that!

Ingredients

For the cream

  • Sour cream - 500g
  • Sugar - 200g
  • Honey - 30g

For the dough

  • Butter - 150g + 40 for the vessel
  • Eggs - 3 pieces
  • Sugar - 150g
  • Honey - 150g
  • Baking Soda - 1 ts
  • Flour - 500g

Instructions

  1. Cut the butter into cubes and leave it at room temperature for 30 minutes until softened.
  2. Beat the eggs with sugar using a mixer until light and fluffy. Add the softened butter and beat thoroughly again until smooth.
  3. Place the honey in a small saucepan. Heat it, stirring, until it comes to a boil. Remove from the heat and immediately add the baking soda. Stir well. Be careful: the mixture will start to foam and rise.
  4. Add the honey mixture to the egg mixture and stir to combine. Gradually add the flour, half a cup at a time, mixing well after each addition. The dough for the Medovik layers should be fairly soft.
  5. Shape the dough into a ball, wrap it in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for 30–40 minutes. After chilling, the dough will be easier to shape when making the classic Medovik layers.
  6. Remove the dough from the refrigerator and divide it into 8–10 portions. Grease a 22–24 cm baking pan with a small amount of butter. Place one portion of dough into the pan and spread it as thinly as possible over the entire surface. Bake at 200°C (390°F) for about 3 minutes, until lightly browned. Prepare all the Medovik layers in the same way.
  7. Prepare the sour cream frosting for the classic Medovik cake. Beat the well-chilled sour cream until fluffy, gradually adding the sugar and honey. The finished frosting should be smooth and uniform.
  8. Crush one cake layer finely using a blender or a rolling pin. Stack the remaining layers, spreading the prepared frosting between them. Use the frosting to cover the top and sides of the Medovik as well.
  9. Sprinkle the cake with the crumbs on all sides and leave it at room temperature for 2 hours. Then refrigerate for 6–8 hours. Decorate as desired before serving.
